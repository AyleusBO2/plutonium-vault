ABOUT PLUTONIUM VAULT

Plutonium Vault is a community-driven archive dedicated to custom Black Ops II Plutonium content. Every calling card and emblem featured on this website is created by members of the community and is completely free to download.

OUR MISSION

Our goal is simple. We want one place where Black Ops II players can easily find high-quality animated calling cards, custom emblems and the creators behind them. No searching through Discord servers. No broken download links. No outdated tutorials. Just quality custom content.

WHAT YOU CAN FIND

ANIMATED CALLING CARDS

Custom BO2 calling cards with live previews and downloadable IWI files.

CUSTOM EMBLEMS

Community-made BO2 emblems ready to install in seconds.

CREATOR PAGES

Dedicated creator profiles showcasing everything each creator has contributed.

FREE DOWNLOADS

Every download on Plutonium Vault is completely free.

THE FUTURE

Plutonium Vault will continue growing with new creators, more calling cards, more emblems and additional community content. Our aim is to become the home of Black Ops II custom content for the Plutonium community.
